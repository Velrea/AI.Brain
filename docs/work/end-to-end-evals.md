# End-to-end evals

An integration test of the whole plugin, played through a model: the skills, the MCP server, the document script, and the core module together. A Python generator writes fictional stories, one set at work and one at home, with timelines and documents, and an eval plays them through the plugin as a user would, then checks what was recorded and how questions about it are answered.

## Decided

- **Everything is fictional**, generated by Python, and checked in as a permanent record.
- **Small**: about a hundred entries, enough to exercise every use case once or more, not a load test.
- **A work scenario and a home scenario**, each a story with a timeline.
- **Every use case is exercised**: recording journal entries, creating entities, filing documents into the documents store, correcting entries, merging entities, taking snapshots, and asking questions.
- **Evals, played through a model**, verify the plugin and its scripts work as intended.
- **Answers are asserted against the stories' facts**: questions asked of the data get the right answers.
- **The documents store is checked**: each filed document is where its entry says, and can be navigated to.

## Suggested

- The generator is seeded, so every run produces the same stories, documents, and expected answers.
- The stories hold the facts each question's answer is checked against, beside the questions.
- Assertions cover the Brain folder's state as well as answers: the entries and their links, the merges, and the filed documents and their hashes.
- The plugin's own eval tooling runs the suite, against a fresh Brain folder each time.

## Done when

- A seeded generator produces fictional work and home stories, about a hundred entries, with their documents.
- An eval suite plays them through the plugin with a model: recording, correcting, merging, filing, snapshotting, and asking.
- Assertions check the recorded entries, the documents store, and the answers to questions against the stories' facts.
- The generator, the stories, and the evals are checked in, and the suite runs on demand.
